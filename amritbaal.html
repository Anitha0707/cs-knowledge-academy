Important Terms & Features (LIC Amritbaal Plan 774)

  • Guaranteed Addition (GA): Accrues at ₹80 per ₹1,000 Basic Sum Assured at the end of each policy year throughout the Policy Term.
  • Maturity Benefit: Basic Sum Assured (BSA) plus total accumulated Guaranteed Additions over the full Policy Term.
  • Risk Commencement Rules: For entry age < 8 years, risk commences either after 2 years from Date of Commencement (DOC) or upon reaching 8 years of age, whichever is earlier. For entry age 8+ years, risk starts immediately.
  • Death Benefit Before Risk Commencement: Refund of total premiums paid (excluding taxes/extra premiums) without interest.
  • Death Benefit After Risk Commencement: Sum Assured on Death plus accrued Guaranteed Additions till year of death. In case of paid-up policy, death cover is minimum 105% of total premiums paid.
  • Policy Eligibility Limits: Min Age at Entry: 30 days (0 Yrs); Max Age at Entry: 13 Yrs. Min Maturity Age: 18 Yrs; Max Maturity Age: 25 Yrs. Min Sum Assured: ₹2,00,000.
