Detailed Rider Coverage Provisions

Accidental Death & Disability Benefit (ADDB)

In case of accidental death, an additional Sum Assured equal to the ADDB Cover is paid to the beneficiary. In case of permanent disability due to accident, future premiums are waived and disability benefit payments are disbursed over 10 years. Maximum limit: Basic Sum Assured or ₹1 Crore (whichever is lower).

New Term Assurance Rider

Provides additional life insurance sum assured in case of unfortunate death of the life assured during the policy term, giving enhanced financial security to dependent family members. Maximum limit: Basic Sum Assured or ₹25 Lakhs (whichever is lower). Term Rider available up to a maximum duration of 35 years.
