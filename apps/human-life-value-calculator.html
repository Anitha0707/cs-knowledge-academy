Human Life Value Calculation – Result Summary

Calculation method: Net Rate of Return = [(1 + Interest Rate) ÷ (1 + Inflation Rate) − 1] × 100, using the rates as decimals. The net annual effective rate is converted to its equivalent monthly effective rate. HLV is the present value of the monthly family amount over the remaining months until retirement. Monthly payments are assumed at each month-end. If the net rate is zero, HLV equals the total of the monthly family amounts.
Illustrative calculation. The result depends on the assumptions and information entered.
